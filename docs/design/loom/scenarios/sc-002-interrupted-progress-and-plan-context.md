# SC-002 — Resume an interrupted delivery and understand its plan

**Context:** A product owner returns after interruption to multi-task work spanning specialist sessions/workflows. They need a truthful summary, not a transcript. Assumption: relevant workflow and evidence state is available through conversation or read-only inspection; actual frequency and comprehension are unvalidated.
**Goal:** Reconstruct outcome, progress, dependencies, evidence, and any needed decision without reassembling context.
**Scenario:** The person locates the project/session/workflow in conversation or the read-only dashboard and inspects relevant Objective/Phase/Wave/Task context, accountable role handoffs, and blockers. They do not need to know or select the available agent roster. If planning-only was requested, a reviewed Plan is presented as the planning workflow's intermediate outcome; the parent Objective/product is not described as complete, and no implementation is implied. In a separate recovery branch, an unfinished role-owned task has exhausted bounded dispatches. After returning later, the user sees the exact target, responsible role, stop reason, preserved evidence/work, and whether a prior continuation authorization was consumed. A fresh explicit request is acknowledged for that exact target; Loom reports whether it resumed, remains exhausted, or completed, separately from acknowledging the request.
**Failure / edge condition:** A planned task has no supported execution/decision path for its accountable role; a handoff omits context; projection is stale/conflicted; the continuation outcome is uncertain; or retry makes no progress. Loom exposes the unsupported path as a blocker instead of silently omitting the role or falling back to a generic executor, and identifies the smallest safe next action without making the user do routine roster matching. Do not label an eligible step active, unknown data zero/healthy, a reviewed Plan as product completion, or an attempted test as a pass. A fresh continuation request is not a general restart or repeated authorization; if its consumption/outcome cannot be established, report uncertainty rather than imply another run.
**Observable outcome:** The person distinguishes planning from implementation and product completion; understands who is accountable for planned work and why Loom stopped; knows what is preserved; can identify whether the exact continuation was accepted and its eventual result; and sees remaining evidence/gates without false completion or being asked to coordinate internal specialists.
