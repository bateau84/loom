# US-001 — Explore, refine, and commit to a product outcome

**Actor / context:** A developer or product owner using Loom in an OpenCode conversation, before and during execution.
**Trigger:** An idea, uncertainty, observed defect, or evolving product decision needs discussion.
**Goal:** Explore, challenge, research, or diagnose without accidentally authorizing implementation; then commit when the outcome is clear and refine it during delivery without restarting unaffected work.
**Observable success:** The person can tell whether Loom is discussing/investigating or carrying out a committed change, sees material uncertainty and user-owned choices, and sees refinements incorporated into affected work while unaffected work/evidence remains useful.
