# US-001 — Change direction and finish

**Actor / context:** A developer directing a multi-step product change with completed work and independent review recorded.

**Trigger:** New information changes remaining work, or interruption breaks the current session.

**Goal:** Finish the authorized outcome without rebuilding context, repeating unrelated work or disguising stale proof as current approval.

**Observable success:** The developer can inspect what remains usable, what changed and why, original attempts/reviews, and the actual next action. Loom carries out feasible authorized continuation without routine “continue” prompts. Genuine waits name their owner and resumption condition. Completion names the delivered scope and independent checks; reviewed planning alone is not product delivery.
