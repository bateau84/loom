**Status:** supported role-safe execution for the currently admitted Task kinds is implemented and independently reviewed on this branch. The universal all-subagent Task paths and current OpenCode roster/Loom-insight discovery described below are proposed, not implemented. Nothing here claims merge, CI, or release.

## Derived from and satisfies

- [Accepted Loom Anchor](../../anchors/loom/anchor.md), especially first principles 1–4 and acceptance criteria 3, 6–11, 30 and 33. Criterion 8 governs accountability and unavailable ownership.
- [BR-024](../../requirements/loom/br-024-preserve-authority-ownership-through-handoffs.md) is an accepted behavioral requirement, including universal appropriately typed subagent eligibility, roster discovery, role-native Task/OQ paths and runtime path validation. [BR-021](../../requirements/loom/br-021-preserve-holistic-plan-context.md) supplies related Plan context; the accepted Anchor remains the product authority.
- [Work hierarchy](work-hierarchy.md), [control plane](control-plane.md), and existing scoped review, OQ, evidence, budget, cancellation, and project isolation contracts remain in force.

## Decision question and current boundary

How can a mandatory Plan Task be admitted, dispatched, reviewed and completed by its actual accountable role without interpreting Worker execution, an OQ answer, a fixed earlier route step or a model summary as a specialist decision? The persistent Plan owns semantic decomposition; the control plane owns executable bindings. On this branch `TaskSpec` and semantic Tasks carry role/responsibility; `applyTaskPlan` in `plugins/loom/workflow.ts` builds role-bound steps, handoff Reviewer gates and user-decision waits. `validatePlanRoleFeasibility` and the compiled-Wave review check supported paths; roll-up and receipts are role-aware for those paths. `PLAN_PRODUCER_ROLES` currently supports Worker, Designer, Specifier, Architect, Documenter, Research and Diagnostic; Reviewer has a review Task path and user-owned decisions have waits. Brainstorm (present in `agents/brainstorm.md`), Planner, Critic and Acceptance are not yet supported as planned Task recipients; Brainstorm also has no currently supported Loom OQ responder path. Other roles may have fixed route gates and/or OQ paths independently of planned Task support. A compiled role label alone never establishes a missing path.

This design covers Objective-depth Plan Tasks and their bounded Wave execution. Simple non-Plan maintenance remains on its route-built path. No change here authorizes new product meaning, substitutes a user decision, or retroactively reclassifies old history. The remaining design extends a working role-safe subset rather than treating the runtime as Worker-only or claiming unimplemented universal routing works already.

## Forces and alternatives

| Option | Mechanism and advantage | Cost, failure and authority boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A. Dedicated contribution for each planned Task (selected; supported-role subset implemented on this branch) | Bind each Task to one exact role-owned `task:<id>` step; Worker Tasks keep their existing step identity. Review Tasks bind to a scoped reviewer gate; user decisions to a non-agent wait. A specialist-to-Worker transfer is two Tasks linked by a reviewed dependency, unless specialist authority was already discharged and evidenced before planning. Attribution, freshness and completion remain one-to-one. | Additional role steps and intermediate gates for cross-role dependencies; Plan schema, compiler, roll-up and projections must move together for each added role-native kind. Cost is bounded by current per-Wave Task limits and existing dispatch budgets. Failure to instantiate the correct role/gate blocks admission. |
| B. Reuse fixed route steps when they appear related, otherwise create role steps | May avoid another specialist invocation when already-routed work is genuinely the planned deliverable. | Requires an exclusive, versioned Task-to-fixed-step binding, evidence partitioning, and review provenance across a step potentially completed before the Plan existed; shared steps are especially ambiguous. Reopen and amendment must not reinterpret prior authority. If strict enough to be safe, it offers little advantage over recording already-satisfied obligations and using A for new work. |

Both can implement real role paths; A is the smaller complete mechanism once evidence/attempt/review isolation and recovery are counted. Do not implement B opportunistically. An earlier reviewed specialist result may instead be cited as **already satisfied** in the obligation map with actual authority/evidence and reviewer-checkable provenance, not attached to a newly minted Task as its completion. If it does not exactly discharge the obligation, plan the remaining specialist work. A role label without dispatch or a promise to add a route later is not a viable third option.

For discovery, compare two viable inputs: (1) host roster plus separate Loom advisory insights (selected), versus (2) an authoritative `.loom` roster or custom agent frontmatter. The latter may be convenient to maintain but diverges when agents are added/removed, confuses preference with executable authority, and custom `loom:` frontmatter risks being forwarded into OpenCode provider options. The selected design asks the host for the current available agents and descriptions; Loom owns only its planning advice and path validation. A missing or stale insight cannot hide an available Loom role or authorize an unsupported path. A third option, inferring paths from a textual description alone, fails the permission/gate boundary.

## Structure and contracts

1. **Semantic Plan.** Each planned Task has one accountable role and bounded role-native kind/outcome inside accepted authority and the currently available host roster. The current `produce`/`execute`/`review`/`obtain-user-decision` responsibilities remain compatible for supported paths; they do not imply that a Planner plan mutation, a Critic challenge, or Product Acceptance can be represented safely by generic `produce`. Keep role/responsibility and any further kind needed for native gates in immutable Plan revisions, not just executable TaskSpec. Multi-role work is decomposed into separate Tasks with explicit handoff edges; a single Task cannot silently change owner halfway through. The handoff names the source result/evidence, receiver's remaining work, and dependent acceptance condition. Routine work may be owned directly by Worker. An obligation marked already-satisfied requires an exact prior result and accepted-authority provenance; `authorized-defer`/out-of-scope uses its existing authority check and is not a runnable completion.
2. **Feasibility versus execution.** Whole-Plan planning-only `review-plan` checks **each** mandatory accepted obligation, including Tasks in later Waves, against a **currently implemented and enabled** route for its named role/responsibility and required independent gate, or an explicit supported user-question/acceptance path. The feasibility check uses the same control-plane role/capability registry and supported slot/decision kinds as delivery admission; it proves the route can represent attribution, dependent blocking and review without creating any executable step or reservation now. It records the exact semantic Plan fingerprint and feasibility receipt; no Task slot, Wave claim or execution authority is created. A proposed design, hypothetical future agent, or Worker-only compiler is not a supported route. If a later Wave contains a mandatory specialist Task whose role is named but unavailable, unrepresentable by this runtime, or lacks its required gate, the Reviewer identifies the exact obligation/Task/path and **refuses planning-only PASS now**, rather than deferring discovery to Wave admission. Delivery rechecks capabilities and exact current Plan at Wave admission; prior planning-only PASS never authorizes a Task. If capability disappears, stop affected admission and report the exact missing role/path.
3. **Executable Wave binding.** Planner's compiled current-Wave contract repeats role/responsibility from semantic Task and identifies its supported route, dependencies, verification and starting scope where relevant. Control plane validates equality against current Plan generation and Wave fingerprint, enabled agent capability, authorized responsibility, downstream independent review, and dependency acyclicity. In one serialized unclaimed-Wave transition it creates a pending `task:<id>` step with `agent=<role>` for producer/executor Tasks, a distinct Reviewer gate over named producer results for review Tasks, or an explicit non-agent decision wait for a genuinely user-owned item. It persists Task ID → exact step/gate/wait, role, responsibility, generation, Wave, semantic fingerprint and gate scope. No slot implies no admission. Dynamic role steps use the same attachment, scope, grant, observation, commit, budget and cancellation fences as fixed steps; they are not run through privileged generic Worker delegation. Decision waits grant no agent permission; General presents and records only the user's actual answer/acceptance on its existing exact user-authority path.
4. **Independent pre-execution review.** `review-plan` receives whole Plan and complete compiled role/slot graph with actual current-Wave contracts. Reviewer attachment binds to review attempt, generation, semantic Plan revision/fingerprint and executable graph fingerprint **including role, responsibility, slot type, gate edges and scope**. PASS revalidates values and enabled capabilities under Wave-claim transaction; FAIL leaves Wave unclaimed and nodes unrunnable. Compiler output alone never claims a Wave. An unrelated unclaimed Wave amendment may preserve an unchanged admitted Wave fingerprint; an affected Wave amendment, reopening review, or invalidation revokes unclaimed binding/verdict. No authority check relies only on a mutable prompt label.
5. **Result and handoff.** An agent's result is tied to exact attached step attempt and current Plan binding, with summary, evidence references and durable artifact/decision reference where applicable. Producer completion records a *result*, not reviewed Task satisfaction. A downstream Task consuming another role's contribution depends on that result **and its independent review gate** (bounded per-contribution gate, or preceding Wave gate when handoff is between Waves). A review Task is the named gate over other work, with independent reviewer attachment, evidence and pass/fail verdict; it cannot review its own output. If a Reviewer-authored deliverable must itself be checked, another eligible independent gate must be available or admission blocks. A Task's persistent `complete` transition occurs only when its bound result/decision and required gate/evidence are satisfied; `syncWorkTaskStatuses` must not mark a role Task complete merely because a producer `loom_complete` returned. OQ answers/reconciliation may resolve narrow questions but cannot close a Task, user wait or gate. Answered blocking OQs retain per-consumer reconciliation; aggregate closure is not a prerequisite for an already reconciled consumer.
6. **Roll-up.** Scoped Wave review (`review-implementation` for a Worker-bearing Wave; truthful role-work Wave review when there is no Worker) depends on every bound Task and required earlier gate, then records an exact reviewed-Wave receipt covering full current-generation Task set and bindings. Do not infer completion from zero Worker steps or `plannedTaskSteps` returning empty. Claims release only after scoped PASS; later knowledge/Objective gates consume receipt rather than reclaiming Wave. Phase/Objective closure retains independent ancestor checks, Product Acceptance when applicable and final review. Planning-only PASS ends only its planning workflow and leaves Objective incomplete. User decision Task remains blocked until exact user-owned act is recorded; General/Reviewer prose is not a substitute.

### Runtime roster, advisory insights and native paths (remaining design)

- **Discover without granting.** The control plane obtains the effective current OpenCode subagent names/descriptions from the host and exposes a bounded read-only roster view to General and Planner. A Loom-owned `.loom` configuration registry may carry planning insights keyed by agent name (native responsibilities, useful task types, cautions); it is optional and advisory. Merge the views so an unlisted but currently available Loom subagent remains visible. Names and descriptions from host/config are untrusted routing inputs: they cannot expand role authority or bypass dispatch permissions, and stale entries are identified rather than mistaken for installed agents. No `loom:` agent frontmatter and no duplicate roster of purportedly installed agents. Refresh host availability on admission/dispatch, not solely when a plan is drafted. General remains the primary coordinator, not a subagent whose capability can be inferred from a roster entry.
- **Separate eligibility from admissibility.** Every currently available Loom subagent is a candidate for appropriately typed Plan Tasks and OQs; the control plane's implemented path descriptors are the authority-bearing side. A descriptor binds a role and *kind of work* to a supported step or decision route, role-owned result and required gate/dependency/evidence contract. Merely being in the roster, choosing an advisory suggestion, or setting a broad `produce` label does not instantiate one. Validate semantic-plan feasibility including later Waves, compiled-Wave binding, exact-role grant/attachment, and task completion against the same installed-path capability and current generation. OQ creation/dispatch/answer separately validates the chosen responder's actual answer path and per-consumer reconciliation; the OQ route never yields a Task receipt or gate verdict. A role without a suitable installed path stays blocked with its named Task/OQ and missing gate/capability, for replanning or later capability enablement; no implicit Worker fallback.
- **Preserve native operations.** Keep ordinary implementation as Worker execution; Designer/Specifier/Architect produce their own authority artifacts; Documenter maintains knowledge; Research and Diagnostic contribute bounded evidence; Reviewer owns an independent scoped verdict over specified work. Brainstorm may own bounded advisory exploration with alternatives, assumptions and trade-offs for a named receiving authority; it does not decide product/design/behavior/architecture, mutate the repository, implement, or turn an idea into an accepted obligation. The receiving authority must explicitly assess or resolve the advisory contribution and supply its own decision/deliverable before dependent work treats meaning as established. Additional kinds must express Planner's bounded Plan creation/amendment with persistent version fencing and separate `review-plan` (a Planner Task cannot self-approve or treat its output as a Wave claim); Critic's independent holistic challenge with existing selectivity/budget and no self-review; and Acceptance's real scenario-plan/results plus product-owned observed proof, not a generic completion summary. These are *proposed additional planned Task paths*, not substitutes for existing fixed gates and not automatically additive gates on routine Tasks. If a role-native output itself requires independent checking, specify an actually independent eligible gate or block admission; avoid a circular Planner Task depending on its own plan, and avoid Reviewer reviewing its own contribution. A specialist's result may hand off to Worker only through the reviewed dependency; OQ answers are not a substitute for any of these outputs.
- **Failure and recovery.** Host roster drift, disabled agent, malformed insight, unsupported Task/OQ kind, missing independent gate, invalid role dependency, stale Plan version, or failed dispatch leaves the exact path unresolved; never mark completed from text, an empty Worker list, a prior fixed-route step, or an OQ answer. A changed role/kind or gate graph requires revalidation/review of affected unclaimed work; claimed bindings remain immutable, and reopen/invalidate resets only affected satisfaction and downstream receipts while preserving history. Keep eligibility of added agents separate from authorization of claimed historical work.

| Accountable subagent | Role-native Task result / prerequisite and gate | Planned Task path on this branch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worker | Bounded implementation; accepted authority and any preceding specialist result/review, then scoped Wave review | Supported |
| Designer, Specifier, Architect | Respective design, behavioral, structural output; independent review before a cross-role consumer | Supported producer paths |
| Documenter | Current-reality documentation/OKF verification after represented work; scoped review | Supported producer path |
| Research, Diagnostic | Bounded sourced facts or causal evidence; independent check before dependent authority consumes it | Supported producer paths |
| Brainstorm | Bounded advisory alternatives for a named receiving authority; scoped independent check of attribution and limits before cross-role use, then the receiving authority's own decision/deliverable. Neither an advisory Task result nor an OQ answer is acceptance or authority resolution | Not yet supported (Task or OQ responder) |
| Reviewer | Scoped independent verdict over named dependencies; cannot review own output | Supported review Task path |
| Planner | Bounded Plan/replan artifact with versioned authority and separate `review-plan`; cannot self-authorize implementation | Not yet supported |
| Critic | Fresh holistic adversarial verdict on the applicable assembled solution/product boundary, within Critic budget; not a routine producer review | Not yet supported |
| Acceptance | Product-owned scenario results and observed proof on the assembled path, before Objective closure; not a prose PASS | Not yet supported |

Each row's OQ path is separate: the named role should be able to answer an appropriately typed narrow question, subject to its own authority and consumer reconciliation, but that answer cannot discharge the row's Task output or gate. For Brainstorm this means an advisory exploration question, never product acceptance or a specialist decision; the current OQ responder allowlist must be extended and validated before dispatch, rather than silently routing the question to Worker/General or claiming it answered. The table describes *planned Task* support, not availability of existing route-built specialist steps or a promise that every task is valid for every role.

### Concrete implementation seams

- Existing `plugins/loom/work.ts` Plan role/responsibility, validation, amendment/history and focused context are the baseline; extend only as necessary for native kinds, and keep current revision/obligation provenance. Do not use `write` or advisory insights as role inference.
- Extend `plugins/loom/tasks.ts` supported paths and `plugins/loom/workflow.ts` compiler/fingerprint/runnable/reopen handling together for new native kinds. Preserve existing Worker Task IDs and supported specialist bindings; status and lifecycle must use the exact Task binding, never `task:<id>` prefix or Worker count as proof of role. Expose a read-only host roster plus separate Loom planning-insight view; do not conflate it with the enforced runtime path descriptor.
- Extend `plugins/loom/index.ts` `loom_task_plan`, OQ responder routing, attachment, `review-plan`, `loom_complete`, claim/sync/receipt, status and exact-role dispatch checks together; update `plugins/loom/lifecycle.ts` history recovery and projections for the added kinds. Receipts remain bound to role/kind/gate graph and Task IDs. UI projections show accountable role, unmet decision/gate and blocked reason, but confer no authority.
- Keep control-plane persistence project-scoped and transaction/version fenced. Review gate must not PASS against executable fingerprint different from attachment. Dispatch grant uses *bound step role*, not caller's claimed identity; stale sessions and role impersonation fail at attachment/commit. Default artifact permissions and runtime elevation remain bounded by assigned role's authority.

## Coverage, failure and compatibility

- Routine Worker Task keeps `task:<id>` role and needs no extra specialist gate merely for ceremony; scoped Wave review still applies. Specialist producer → Worker in **same Wave** requires intermediate independent gate before Worker runs, not only final Wave review. Between Waves reviewed predecessor Wave receipt supplies edge. Role-only Wave is not a zero-Task Wave; its actual steps and scoped review count. Reviewer Task names output it independently judges. User decision/Anchor acceptance uses real user-authority record, not synthetic gate.
- Invalid owner, nonexistent/disabled role, unsupported responsibility, missing gate, ambiguous handoff, failed dispatch or unavailable user path: leave relevant work pending/blocked with exact cause; neither default to Worker/General nor auto-PASS. Recompile only unclaimed/unconsumed work after correction. Failed producer or denied grant cannot create satisfying receipt.
- Claimed Waves keep immutable admitted role/graph contract. Newly discovered authority work stops dependent progress and routes Plan amendment for still-unclaimed work; changed already-consumed premise requires invalidation/new generation or explicit compatible carry-forward proof. Reopen resets affected step attempts, dependent gates, evidence proof, Task satisfaction and Wave receipt; unaffected reviewed work remains historical. Concurrent claim attempts are serialized/version-checked; only one acquires exact Wave. Cancellation revokes new admission/grants but cannot erase in-flight operations or preserved evidence.
- Existing Worker-only claimed/completed Waves remain legible on historical contract. They are **not** migrated into specialist proof or silently converted to role-only execution. Previously unclaimed Plan Tasks lacking roles cannot be admitted by new compiler until Planner supplies roles in a fresh permitted revision and review succeeds. An old claim that cannot safely be reinterpreted remains historical; replan at next unclaimed boundary. Runtime version fencing prevents older writers from issuing Worker-only compilation into new role-aware state. An all-complete legacy Worker Wave is not evidence of a formerly unrepresented specialist decision.

## Invariants and falsification

| Guarantee and precondition | Enforcement boundary | Falsifying observation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Each mandatory Task in admitted Wave has one current role/responsibility and exact supported slot/wait before claim. | Plan validator + compiler + `review-plan`/claim transaction. | Missing/disabled role or slot receives PASS/claim; owner inferred from write scope. |
| Receiving role never consumes unreviewed specialist result or invented user act. | Handoff DAG + independent gate/user-authority record + runtime runnable/Task transition. | Worker or General completion advances dependent Task while source gate/user wait pending. |
| Reviewed role output is attributed to current generation, Task, step attempt and independent gate. | Binding/receipt fingerprints + exact session attachment/completion. | Old OQ answer, step, Plan revision or stale session makes new Task complete. |
| No-Worker Wave and planning-only completion remain distinct from Objective completion. | Wave roll-up and Objective ancestor gates. | Empty Worker list auto-completes Wave/Objective or planning-only PASS starts execution. |
| Scope, project identity and concurrency cannot bypass authority. | Project storage, serialized claim, role-aware grants/mutation hooks. | Cross-project binding, competing claims, or Worker grant runs specialist slot. |
| A roster/insight entry informs selection but never admits a Task or OQ. | Host discovery + separate advisory registry + control-plane path/gate validation at admission and dispatch. | A configured preference authorizes a missing role path; an unlisted available Loom subagent disappears from candidate discovery. |
| Each new role-native Task kind completes only from its own required result and independent gates. | Brainstorm/Planner/Reviewer/Critic/Acceptance-specific bindings, observed evidence, Plan version and scoped gate transitions. | Brainstorm advice becomes accepted meaning, Planner's draft claims its Wave, an OQ answer satisfies a Task, or a Critic/Acceptance summary substitutes for required verdict/scenario evidence. |

## Downstream proof and reconsideration

For the **remaining implementation**, run deterministic control-plane tests and real attached-tool integration for: host roster/descriptions and Loom planning insights visible to both General and Planner, unlisted-but-available Loom subagent eligibility, stale/absent/malformed advisory entries, no `loom:` frontmatter, and a preference that cannot grant a path. Exercise every available Loom subagent with a role-appropriate planned Task and OQ; explicitly cover Brainstorm's advisory Task and advisory OQ (receiving authority remains accountable and performs its own resolution; unsupported current routes block), Planner versioned planning/replanning without self-approval, Critic bounded holistic verdict, Acceptance real scenario evidence, Reviewer independent scoped gate, and the existing producer-role subset. For each, test absent agent, unsupported kind/gate, invalid dependency, stale roster/Plan/attachment and OQ responder path, exact blocked reason, and no fallback/false completion. Cover whole-Plan planning-only feasible/infeasible *later-Wave* specialist paths, no Wave claim from planning-only PASS, routine Worker, specialist → Worker same-Wave gate and cross-Wave receipt, user decision without impersonation, role-only Wave/Objective, concurrent claims, reopen, cancellation, failed dispatch, legacy Worker-only history and no retroactive specialist credit. Assert persisted Task/step/role/kind/gate/receipt state, actual host dispatch denial and observed proof—not just Plan text or model claims. These are downstream proof requirements, **not** claims that the universal paths or roster tooling already pass; existing supported-path review does not cover them. Reconsider A only if measured bounded Wave limits or demonstrably safe reuse requires B, with equivalent isolation/compatibility proof. No unresolved product-policy choice is introduced by this technical design.
