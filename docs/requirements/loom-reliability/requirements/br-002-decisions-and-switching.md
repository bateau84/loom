# BR-002 — Revise decisions and switch work without fake acceptance

**Statement:**
Loom shall support decision amendment, intent abandonment and pause/switch/return as ordinary authorized transitions without requiring acceptance of obsolete meaning or completion of unrelated work.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- An unasked planned decision can be amended/removed by its authorized Plan owner subject to applicable review; no obsolete answer or user decision is manufactured.
- Once asked, original question/answer identity and provenance remain. Amendment identifies replacement and affected consumers; the old answer applies only if it answers the current decision under OC-002. Silence is not consent. Changed user-owned meaning needs the user's answer; ordinary specialist choices stay specialist-owned.
- Abandonment with an unanswered question retains interview history but does not accept its Anchor. Different authorized work proceeds in the same conversation without approval/completion of the original goal.
- Before switching active A to unrelated B, confirm A's exact admission pause boundary: no new A work starts until resumed. Previously admitted operations remain explicitly running/uncertain as appropriate; binding is not quiescence.
- User-authorized cancellation of B permits its authorized owner to return to A without dummy B success, recreated history or mandatory new Objective. Access restoration is not execution permission; A's current prerequisites still apply.
- Corrections affect current meaning/dependent applicability, never rewrite originally consumed meaning or automatically approve new gates.

**Verification semantics:**
Exercise unasked and asked amendment, unanswered intent abandonment and A-pause/B-start/B-cancel/A-return with an in-flight A operation. Observe original records, exact admission boundaries and resumed prerequisites. Obsolete answers, unwanted acceptance or side-work success required solely to unlock return falsify this requirement.
