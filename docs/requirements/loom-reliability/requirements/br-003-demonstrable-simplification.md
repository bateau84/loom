# BR-003 — Demonstrable removal of duplicate authority

**Statement:**
Loom shall demonstrably remove duplicated maintained facts, redundant decisions and unnecessary coordination while retaining distinct execution, applicability, independent review and product acceptance facts.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- Plan meaning has one authoritative source. Execution obtains its exact Task contract without a second model-authored copy. Genuine execution settings may be separately owned but cannot redefine Task meaning.
- Every authoritative record identifies its unique non-derivable fact/owner. Rebuildable views/caches/indexes are non-authoritative; disagreements are repaired from owning facts, not independently editing both copies.
- Before/after comparison identifies duplicate writable facts/decisions actually removed, surviving writers, derivations and eliminated coordination. Relocation, renaming or a facade hiding unchanged contradictory bookkeeping does not pass.
- Rebuilding projections cannot manufacture/erase original execution/evidence, user consent, independent judgment or acceptance; these cannot be derived from producer success or each other.
- Both gates stand independently: reduction cannot excuse failed journeys and working journeys cannot substitute for actual removal.

**Verification semantics:**
Independently inspect before/after fact ownership/write paths and observe projection reconstruction and Plan-to-execution handoff. Show concrete removals without replacement overlapping authority, separately from completed reliability traces. LOC, file or tool-call counts alone are insufficient.
