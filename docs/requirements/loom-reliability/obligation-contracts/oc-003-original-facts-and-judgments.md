# OC-003 — Original facts and independent judgments survive change

**Participants:**
- Producers/operation observers, work/evidence/decision preservers/restorers, independent assessors and dependent-work admission owners.

**Preconditions:**
- Work/operation has been authorized, attempted, observed, assessed or durably acknowledged.

**Guarantees:**
- Plan/workflow/format/code change preserves recoverable attributable historical user work, decisions, original attempts, evidence and reviews, including exact consumed meaning/input context. Correction adds attributable correction/supersession, not replacement of original truth.
- Permission, invocation, observed outcome, result claim, applicability, independent verdict and acceptance remain distinct. Interrupted, failed, missing-proof and unknown records are not upgraded by planning, restore, attachment or projection repair.
- Independent judgments identify subject/context and reviewer/producer. Repairer's verification is evidence, not independent approval of their repair. Changed/reopened review subject needs current independent assessment; original PASS remains historical, not universal approval.
- Shared-file authorship is honest through isolation or explicit accountable handoff of actual changes. Scope admission or named-file commit cannot establish ownership of another session's bytes. Mixed/unknown authorship remains unresolved, not claimed.
- Admitted external effects remain observed running/completed/failed/unknown across pause, cancellation, restart and restore. Before potentially duplicating retry, establish outcome or an authorized non-duplicating reconciliation strategy; lost response is not proof of no effect.

**Semantic input/output:**
- Input: original attributed authority, attempts, observations, artifacts and judgments plus proposed use/transition.
- Output: retained originals, separately current applicability/correction/operation reconciliation/new judgment.

**Failure semantics:**
- Late results belong to original attempt/context; new use requires current applicability. Duplicate arrival creates no second execution/review.
- Missing/contradictory proof remains missing/unproven, never reconstructed as observation. Internal restore does not undo external effects or grant foreign-work ownership.

**Invariant:**
- Originals remain truthful/attributable when inapplicable; admission stop does not imply external quiescence.

**Verification semantics:**
Observe changed contracts, repair/re-review, mixed-file edits, lost response, duplicate late callbacks and restore. Retrieve unchanged originals and separate current use. Deny self-approval and false authorship without deleting history or trapping feasible continuation.
