# Historical handback experiment

The earlier direct-bridge handback experiment was a test-only feasibility probe,
not the current collaboration model. Its successful runs and scripted provider
results do not prove the replacement protocol. Do not transplant its completion
bridge, raw synthetic prompts or scenario counts into production acceptance.

Current [questions](collaboration.md) fail closed for recipient dispatch on the
supported OpenCode 2.0.32 host. Manual native handling remains necessary. Host boot
and installed-question qualification are separate evidence. A first new blocking
answer may attempt one best-effort exact-asker resume; it is not a general
handback engine, assignment route or completion nudge.

See [installed-host validation](installed-host-validation.md) for evidence scope
and [the historical comparison boundary](handback-comparison.md). No command here
authorizes changing the normal host or replaying old state.
