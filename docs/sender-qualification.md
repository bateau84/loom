# Sender qualification: historical boundary

Previous qualification covered the retired default-off answer-to-primary sender,
its consent, withdrawal and uncertain-admission protocol. Those results are
historical; they do not qualify the new first-answer-only exact-asker resume.
Old scenario names/counts must not be relabelled as current acceptance.

Current behavior and failure semantics are in [collaboration](collaboration.md).
Registered callback/SQLite tests can prove persistence and adapter contracts with
a simulated native boundary. They do not prove installed native authority,
genuine ToolContext attribution or consumption by a model.

Current installed acceptance must identify the exact assembled artifact and
unmodified host version, exercise real registered tools and persistence, verify
zero recipient contact during ask, exact asking-Session targeting with unchanged
native agent/model/permissions, firstness and negative paths, and populated
historical preservation/full_thread traversal. Report unavailable variants as
UNPROVEN, not a fabricated pass. See [installed-host validation](installed-host-validation.md).

Admission is not processing/completion. No retries, replay, transfers or eventual
consumption guarantee are offered. This document does not enable a sender,
authorize host restart, paid inference, deployment or activation.
