# Product Lifecycle Assessment

For Reviewer when lifecycle coverage is material to the assigned product outcome. Do not turn the possible paths below into universal requirements.

## Review criteria

- Coverage is derived from the accepted users, environment, lifespan, data, guarantees, and delivery boundary. Optional operational improvements are distinguishable from necessary work and authorized exclusions.
- Applicable setup-to-first-result, continuing-use, recovery, and supported-change paths have meaningful starting conditions and outcomes. An already-configured developer environment is not silently treated as a fresh installation.
- Observable guarantees, human-facing behavior, structural decisions, and current-reality documentation remain with their respective owners. Suggested rollback, retention, or monitoring policies have not become accepted by implication.
- Necessary work and proof expectations reach the existing decomposition/verification surfaces; missing setup, migration, or recovery work is not lost between component tasks.
- Evidence can distinguish the claimed lifecycle property from a weaker one: restoration versus backup creation, upgrade versus fresh install, actionable recovery versus an error message, and real initialization versus seeded state.
- Reports separate proposed coverage, executed checks, accepted limitations, and readiness claims. No extra operations gate or enterprise process is imposed on a bounded prototype.

Identify the exact accepted path, missing contribution/proof, and correction owner. Preserve valid feature evidence while naming what it cannot establish about lifecycle use.
