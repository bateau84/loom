# design-specification Reviewer Assessment

Reviewer-only domain contract. It does not create Loom authority, product meaning, or proof.

## Review criteria

- Human-facing flows, states, feedback, recovery, accessibility, and visible consequences are explicit enough to implement consistently.
- Design owns experience, not backend/protocol realization.
- Edge/error/empty/loading states are covered when users can encounter them.
- Interaction choices trace to accepted goals rather than aesthetic preference alone.
- Accessibility requirements are integrated into the interaction, not appended as generic advice.

## Review depth

Scale review depth with consequence, uncertainty, boundary count, and blast radius.
