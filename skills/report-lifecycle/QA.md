# report-lifecycle Quality Assurance

## QA criteria

- Attack reports that look valid but misstate producer, verdict, evidence, uncertainty, or provenance.
- Look for report proliferation where in-session output was sufficient.
- Look for durable-document smuggling, authority elevation, specialist attempts to perform General-only promotion, or manual promotion outside the retention path.
- Treat OKF validity as structural correctness only, never semantic approval.
