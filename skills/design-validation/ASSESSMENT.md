# design-validation Reviewer Assessment

Reviewer-only domain contract. It does not create Loom authority, product meaning, or proof.

## Review criteria

- Validation observes the realized experience rather than judging design artifacts alone.
- Accepted flows, visible states, feedback, recovery, accessibility, and critical edge states are exercised.
- Findings distinguish implementation defects from missing/changed design authority.
- Evidence captures what a user can actually perceive/do.
- Validation does not silently redefine product behavior to fit implementation.

## Review depth

Scale review depth with consequence, uncertainty, boundary count, and blast radius.
