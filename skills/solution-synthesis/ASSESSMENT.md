# Solution Synthesis Assessment

For Reviewer when cross-domain reconciliation is within the assigned outcome. Assess the producing owners' work without becoming its co-author.

## Review criteria

- Representative journeys connect the accepted outcome to current experience, behavior, capabilities, and structural responsibilities; matching headings or source links alone do not establish agreement.
- Shared terms, identities, states, ownership, ordering, and failure/recovery meanings agree across the affected authoritative artifacts, not merely in the coordinator's summary.
- Resolutions were made by the appropriate owners. Proposed requests, implementation convenience, and review findings have not become new product or specialist authority.
- Required capabilities are supported, explicitly unknown, or identified as demonstrated gaps with a receiving/producing owner. Difficult gaps are not hidden behind mocks or a sent handoff request.
- The proof path reaches the assembled outcome through a meaningful product boundary. Planned evidence, executed evidence, and acceptance judgments remain distinct.
- Changes have reached the affected consumers, while unrelated valid work is retained. There is no competing master specification or unnecessary universal specialist round.

Name the incompatible clauses, missing contribution, or stale consumer and its actual correction owner. Do not rewrite the solution to secure a passing review.
