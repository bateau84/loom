# design-implementation Quality Assurance

## QA criteria

- Swap ideal content for long labels, large/empty collections, stale data, or a failed action. Look for a polished component whose composed journey breaks.
- Follow a shared-token change into another consumer; challenge local visual success that creates distant hierarchy or accessibility regressions.
- Trace an accepted exception through a reused template. Could a generic recipe silently override it while every component appears correct?
- Challenge green tests or screenshots that hide a mock dependency, unobserved focus behavior, an invented completion promise, or an untested recovery path.
- Look for a context-specific direction lost between design, implementation, and the evidence given to review, including stale source references.

## QA depth

Attack correlated confidence and integration gaps. Do not prescribe a new aesthetic or reject harmless implementation variation.
