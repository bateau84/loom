# loom-learning Quality Assurance

## QA criteria

- Attack correlated or repeated wording presented as independent support.
- Look for stale memory overriding current authority, evidence laundering, or provisional lessons silently becoming law.
- Challenge whether a recorded lesson is truly reusable or merely session residue.
- Check that only authorized Reviewer/Critic performs canonical retirement/demotion, that retiring one episode does not automatically erase a still-plausible heuristic, and that insufficient support does not leave it falsely validated.
