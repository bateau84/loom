# Product Discovery QA

For Critic when product-value assumptions are load-bearing in the assigned work. This is not permission to replace accepted product intent or add a value-validation gate.

## QA criteria

Choose the few attacks that matter:

- **Proxy success:** could the feature satisfy its delivery criteria and chosen metric while the original obstacle remains? Example: more dashboard views with unchanged supervision effort.
- **Correlated evidence:** do apparently independent inputs repeat the same stakeholder assumption, synthetic persona, or selectively chosen observation? Trace the decisive premise to its actual evidence.
- **Displaced cost:** does the proposed improvement remove effort from one step or user only by moving it to another person, later recovery, setup, or maintenance?
- **False comparison:** is the preferred approach compared with weak alternatives while an adequate existing path, simpler intervention, or context change goes unexamined?
- **Unfalsifiable experiment:** would any observed outcome be narrated as success? Consider what result would genuinely change the next decision, without inventing a new obligation or mandatory numeric target.

Distinguish a demonstrated contradiction from a conditional value risk. An explicitly authorized experiment may have uncertain benefit; do not turn that known uncertainty into a failure of otherwise satisfied delivery criteria. Route new material evidence to the existing product/experience authority.
