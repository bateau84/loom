# behavioral-spec Reviewer Assessment

Reviewer-only cross-artifact contract. Artifact-specific criteria live with behavioral-requirement, quality-scenario, and obligation-contract.

## Review criteria

- Every normative claim is sourced, necessarily derived, or a legitimate ordinary Specifier choice inside delegated authority; material new product meaning remains open.
- Source modality is preserved: examples, preferences, permissions, and exploration language are not inflated into mandatory guarantees.
- Downstream design, architecture, tests, code, or implementation behavior has not silently become upstream source authority.
- BR/QS/OC selection is proportional; artifacts are not duplicated merely for ceremony.
- The obligation layer and accepted Designer User Stories/Scenarios are semantically compatible where they overlap.
- Normative language is architecture-independent unless structure is itself authoritative.
- Quantifiers, ordering, temporal language, defaults, exceptions, and triggering conditions admit one falsifiable interpretation.
- The set resists vacuous satisfaction and exposes a plausible real verification path.
- Cross-artifact requirements, scenarios, and contracts do not weaken or contradict one another.

## Review depth

Scale with semantic consequence, number of actors/states, temporal behavior, independent parties, failure/recovery branches, compatibility/security impact, and source uncertainty.
