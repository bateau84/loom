# agent-file-authoring Reviewer Assessment

Reviewer-only domain contract. It does not create Loom authority, product meaning, or proof.

## Review criteria

- Authority fidelity: prose matches current accepted Loom authority and does not expand merely because the role can notice a defect.
- Permission/prose parity: tool, filesystem, dispatch, and mutation capability neither exceeds nor contradicts the written role.
- Control-plane coherence: prompt rules do not recreate workflow state, bypass mechanical gates, or invent alternate handoff protocols.
- Independent-gate neutrality: coordinator/producer wording cannot bias Reviewer, Acceptance, or Critic verdicts.
- Stop and escalation conditions are explicit where the role cannot decide, execute, or prove something.
- Behavioral evals discriminate the intended decision boundary rather than checking phrases.
- Negative-space search confirms contradictory old role/gate/tool language is gone.
- Portability: agent directives do not depend on source-repository-local IDs or paths as universal authority.

## Review depth

Scale review depth with consequence, uncertainty, boundary count, and blast radius.
