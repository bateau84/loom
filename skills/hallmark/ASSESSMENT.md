# hallmark Reviewer Assessment

## Review criteria

- The visual direction traces to actual product/user context and accepted authority; proposals and research assumptions are not reported as accepted facts.
- Structure, copy, visual hierarchy, and expressive details implement that direction rather than an unrelated theme or generic page rhythm.
- Real project templates, flows, components, and tokens are identified and used where applicable. Missing or stale references are not replaced by invented capabilities.
- Consequential alternatives are meaningfully different and compared against the same scenarios; settled local work is not forced through a branding exercise.
- Reachable states, accessibility, responsive behavior, and truthful content survive the visual treatment. Generic recipes do not override accepted choices.
- Craft findings state a location, evidence boundary, accepted intent, user/context consequence, and correction. Optional improvements are not promoted to new requirements or arbitrary taste scores.

## Review depth

Scale with the consequence of the visual direction and the journeys affected. A single inherited component may need only a contextual fit check; a new cross-product visual language needs representative composed flows and difficult states.
