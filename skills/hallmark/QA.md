# hallmark Quality Assurance

## QA criteria

- Follow a user between independently polished screens. Look for changes in vocabulary, identity, status meaning, density, or feedback that break the whole journey.
- Remove the proposal's brand name and inspect its actual choices. Challenge generic output only where an accepted contextual need or direction is missing, not because familiar patterns are inherently bad.
- Change the data to long labels, empty/large collections, stale status, and an error. Does the apparent craft depend on perfect demo content or fabricated proof?
- Check whether producer and Reviewer both mistook a screenshot, invented metrics, template names, or a self-awarded score for observed product quality.
- Challenge an impressive novel interaction whose timing, accessibility, recovery, or learning cost was never observed. Equally, challenge rejection based only on unfamiliarity when evidence supports the choice.
- Test whether an inherited recipe silently defeated an accepted exception or allowed uncontrolled shared-token drift.

## QA depth

Concentrate on correlated false confidence and cross-screen composition. Do not introduce a new preferred aesthetic, novelty quota, or open-ended polishing requirement.
